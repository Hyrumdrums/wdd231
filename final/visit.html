Getting There

Carlsbad Caverns National Park is located in southeastern New Mexico, about 30 miles southwest of the city of Carlsbad. The park is easily accessible by car, and there are several routes you can take to get there depending on your starting point.

If you're coming from the north, you can take US Highway 285 south from Roswell or US Highway 62/180 south from Artesia. If you're coming from the south, you can take US Highway 62/180 north from El Paso, Texas. If you're coming from the west, you can take US Highway 62/180 east from Carlsbad.

Accommodations and Best Time to Visit

Once you arrive at the park, there are several options for accommodations. The park has a campground with sites for tents and RVs, as well as a lodge with rooms and suites. There are also several hotels and motels in the nearby city of Carlsbad.

When planning your trip, it's important to consider the time of year you'll be visiting. The park is open year-round, but the best time to visit is during the spring and fall when the weather is mild and the crowds are smaller. Summer can be hot and crowded, while winter can be cold and snowy.

Entering the Caverns

Visiting the caverns is an unforgettable experience. The park offers several tour options, from easy walks to more challenging expeditions. Be sure to check the park's website for current tour schedules and availability.

The basic experience is a self guided tour. You can take the elevator straight to the Big Room, however, if you're capable, the hike down gives you the greatest experience, though it is quite challenging.
